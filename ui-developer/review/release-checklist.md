# Release checklist

Use applicable items only. Every checked item must be supported by inspection or test evidence.

## Brief and direction

- [ ] The artifact, users, primary job, content, and constraints are understood.
- [ ] The surface intention and applicable variation, motion, and density levels were declared for substantial new work.
- [ ] The implemented direction traces back to the brief or established system.
- [ ] The signature move supports the product instead of distracting from it.
- [ ] References were transposed, not cloned.
- [ ] Required real material is present, requested, or represented by explicit placeholders rather than fabricated proof.
- [ ] Scope and preserved behavior remain intact.

## Hierarchy and content

- [ ] The page title and section structure are semantically correct.
- [ ] Visual roles remain distinct across target sizes.
- [ ] Subtitles, eyebrows, labels, and numbers add information rather than template chrome.
- [ ] Copy is concrete, consistent, and user-facing.
- [ ] No factual claims, metrics, testimonials, or product behavior were invented.
- [ ] Long, empty, missing, and extreme content has been checked.

## Components and interaction

- [ ] Primary and secondary actions are correctly ranked.
- [ ] Applicable hover, active, focus, disabled, loading, success, warning, error, selected, and empty states exist.
- [ ] Forms preserve input and provide actionable errors.
- [ ] Navigation, overlays, tables, and disclosures follow expected behavior.
- [ ] Feedback appears at the right time and location.

## Responsive and accessible behavior

- [ ] Narrow, intermediate, and wide layouts were inspected.
- [ ] Text expansion, zoom, wrapping, overflow, and touch use are safe.
- [ ] Keyboard access and focus order were manually checked.
- [ ] Focus is visible and not obscured.
- [ ] Semantics, accessible names, descriptions, and dynamic status are correct.
- [ ] Contrast and non-color cues are adequate.
- [ ] Reduced motion is respected.
- [ ] Automated checks were supplemented by manual assessment.

## Visual craft and specificity

- [ ] Hierarchy works in grayscale and at reduced zoom.
- [ ] Spacing communicates relationships and has intentional rhythm.
- [ ] Typography, icons, imagery, surfaces, and motion form one system.
- [ ] Cards, borders, radius, shadows, and effects have functional or compositional jobs.
- [ ] The bare structure does not collapse into an unrelated category template.
- [ ] First- and second-order generated-UI tells do not accumulate without rationale.
- [ ] Heuristic source-scan findings were reviewed in context when the scanner was applicable.
- [ ] The render was rechecked after final corrections.

## Engineering and performance

- [ ] Existing architecture and public behavior were preserved unless change was requested.
- [ ] New dependencies, fonts, or assets are justified and disclosed.
- [ ] Relevant lint, type, unit, component, end-to-end, and build checks pass.
- [ ] Broken assets, console errors, failed requests, and hydration issues were checked.
- [ ] Images, fonts, client code, animations, and layout stability are proportionate.
- [ ] Visual baselines were reviewed rather than blindly updated.

## Handoff

- [ ] Outcome and design direction are summarized.
- [ ] Changed surfaces or files are identified.
- [ ] Validation evidence is reported.
- [ ] Remaining limitations, assumptions, and unverified areas are explicit.
